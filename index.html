Emre Can Acikgoz is a PhD student in Computer Science at UIUC, advised by Gokhan Tur and Dilek Hakkani-Tür. He is also currently a research intern at Microsoft Research, supervised by Baolin Peng and Jianfeng Gao. His research focuses on self-evolving LLM agents and agentic learning, with interests in post-training, self-play, and test-time scaling. His work has been published at top-tier venues including NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL, and EMNLP; representative works include ToolRL, CoALM, TT-SI, and Tool-R0. He co-organizes Lifelong Agents: Learning, Aligning, and Evolving at ICLR 2026 and COLM 2026. He is co-chair of the Student Research Workshop (SRW) at ACL 2027.

Project Lifelong Agent

The broader vision shaping my PhD is agents that continually learn, adapt, and generalize with minimal supervision, and that keep learning after deployment. The project has three pillars: Learning, Aligning, and Evolving. I focus primarily on Evolving: how agents can adapt themselves to new environments through experience, self-play, and test-time training.
